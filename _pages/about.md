I am a PhD student in [Earth System Science](https://earthsystemscience.stanford.edu/) at Stanford University. I am advised by [Steve Luby](https://lubylab.stanford.edu/), [Gabrielle Wong-Parodi](https://decisionsenvironment.stanford.edu/), and [Beatriz Magaloni-Kerpel](https://povgov.com/). I am a Knight-Hennessy Scholar, National Geographic Explorer, and Google Developer Expert.

My research seeks to build public health resilience to climate shocks in humanitarian settings using remote sensing and social epidemiological methods. I focus much of my research in South Asia, partnering with regional public health and civil society organizations. I also work to build global remote sensing capacity among low-resourced institutions in climate-vulnerable regions.

Previously, I received my MSc in Climate Change and Planetary Health at London School of Hygiene and Tropical Medicine, where I was a Marshall Scholar. I received my BS in Earth Systems at Stanford University. I also served as a biospheric scientist at NASA for nine years.
